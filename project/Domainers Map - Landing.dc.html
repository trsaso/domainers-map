How it works

Not pins on a map — a path from a name you hold to a company that needs it.

01

Map the market

Investors, brokers, startups and events, clustered by region and filterable by TLD, focus and verification. City-level only — nobody's doorstep.

02

Match inventory to buyers

Each domain is scored against companies on wish-list, brand-name and keyword fit plus a budget signal — with the reasons shown, never a black box.

03

Work the lead

One click turns a match into a lead. Track stage and notes in a pipeline built for name sales, not generic CRM.
